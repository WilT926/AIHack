Dot Grid (1a 1c 1d 1f) borrows your reference directly: a strict grid of discs that melt into each other. Ink (1b 1e) is outline-only, like pen on paper. SECTION 2

Participatory design in studio teaching

Studio pedagogy has long relied on the crit as its central ritual. Scholars describe it as both formative and, at times, adversarial, a space where students learn to defend decisions in public.

Recent work reframes the review as a conversation rather than a verdict. Participatory models invite students to set the agenda, choosing which questions they want the panel to address first.

However, few studies measure how rehearsal changes confidence on the day. This gap motivates the present study.
